Privacy Policy

KNEXM Co., Ltd. · Effective November 1, 2026

This is a translation for convenience. In case of any difference, the Korean version prevails.

KNEXM Co., Ltd. (the “Company”) values the personal information of its users and complies with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea. This policy explains how personal information is handled on our website (knexm.com) and when you contact us.

1. Information we collect

We may receive the following information when you contact us by email or phone, or use our website.

  • Email inquiries: name, email address, contact number and message (information you send us directly)
  • Website access logs: IP address, browser type and access time (records kept automatically by our hosting service)

Our website has no sign-up, login or payment features, and we do not use our own cookies or visitor-tracking tools.

2. How we use it

We use personal information only for the following purposes.

  • Reviewing and replying to inquiries and partnership proposals
  • Keeping the website stable and secure

3. Retention and deletion

Personal information is deleted without delay once its purpose has been fulfilled. Where the law requires us to keep it for a certain period, we store it securely for that period and then delete it. Electronic files are erased so they cannot be recovered, and paper documents are shredded or incinerated.

4. Disclosure to third parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties, except where specifically required by law or requested by investigative authorities following legal procedures.

5. Service providers

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., and access logs may be stored on GitHub’s servers outside Korea. We also load fonts from jsDelivr and show Google Maps on the Location page, so basic connection information such as your IP address may be sent to those services when you open a page. Each service handles personal information under its own policy.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time. Please contact us below and we will act without delay.
